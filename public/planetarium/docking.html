Berthing

Canadarm2 captures the vehicle. The crew bolts it to a Common Berthing Mechanism. That is not docking.

the arm catches the ship and the crew bolts it on

Propellant

  • Progress transfers hypergolic propellant (UDMH / NTO) into the Russian segment through the docking interface. the cargo ship that also fills the Russian tanks
  • Crew Dragon does not transfer propellant into station tanks. people and cargo; it does not fill station tanks
  • Cygnus can reboost by firing its own engine while berthed. The propellant stays aboard Cygnus. a push from the ship’s own engine; the propellant stays aboard Cygnus
