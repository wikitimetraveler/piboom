Quick Start

  1. Click the 🎤 microphone button on any voice-enabled page
  2. Allow microphone access when prompted by your browser
  3. Speak your command clearly
  4. Wait for the system to respond

Pro Tip

Speak naturally! The AI understands conversational language. You don't need to use exact commands.

Tips & Tricks

Best Practices

  • Speak clearly in a quiet environment
  • Wait for the listening indicator before speaking
  • Use natural, conversational language
  • Be specific with artist names and album titles
  • Try different phrasings if not understood

Keyboard Shortcuts

  • Escape - Stop assistant from speaking
  • Enter - Send message in chat

Troubleshooting

Common Issues:

❌ "Microphone not working"
Solution: Check browser permissions. Click the lock icon in the address bar and enable microphone access.
❌ "Voice not recognized"
Solution: Speak clearly and in a quiet environment. Try rephrasing your command.
❌ "Assistant won't stop talking"
Solution: Press the Escape key or click the "Stop Speaking" button.
