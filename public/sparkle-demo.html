✨ Sparkle Fall Effect ✨

Beautiful animated sparkles falling across your page

Customize Your Sparkles

How to Add to Any Page

Method 1: Automatic (easiest)

Add the script and the data-sparkle-fall attribute to your body tag:

<body data-sparkle-fall>
    <!-- your content -->
    <script src="/shared/sparkle-fall.js"></script>
</body>

Method 2: Custom JavaScript

Create an instance with custom options:

<script src="/shared/sparkle-fall.js"></script>
<script>
    const sparkles = new SparkleFall({
        particleCount: 80,
        colors: ['#FFD700', '#FFF', '#FFE4B5'],
        minSpeed: 1,
        maxSpeed: 3,
        minSize: 2,
        maxSize: 6
    });
</script>
