DTI Guidelines:
• Conventional: Front-End ≤ 28%, Back-End ≤ 36% (up to 45% with compensating factors)
• FHA: Front-End ≤ 31%, Back-End ≤ 43% (up to 57% with compensating factors)
• VA: Back-End typically ≤ 41% (no front-end requirement)
• USDA: Front-End ≤ 29%, Back-End ≤ 41%
