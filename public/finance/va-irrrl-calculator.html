What is a VA IRRRL?
An Interest Rate Reduction Refinance Loan (IRRRL) is a VA-backed refinance loan that helps you lower your monthly payment by reducing your interest rate. It's a simple, streamlined process - no appraisal needed, no income verification, and you can roll closing costs into your loan.

VA Funding Fee

VA Funding Fee: The VA charges a funding fee for IRRRL loans, typically 0.5% of the loan amount. This fee can be waived if you receive VA disability compensation or if you're a surviving spouse of a veteran who died in service or from a service-connected disability.
