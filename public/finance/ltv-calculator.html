Mortgage Insurance Requirements

Mortgage Insurance Guidelines:
• Conventional: PMI required if LTV > 80%. Can be removed at 78% LTV (automatic) or 80% LTV (request)
• FHA: MIP required for all loans. Annual MIP for life of loan if LTV > 90% and term > 15 years
• VA: No MI required (VA funding fee instead)
• USDA: Guarantee fee required (similar to MI)
