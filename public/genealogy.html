Genealogy API Endpoints

Use these API endpoints to integrate genealogy data:

GET /api/genealogy/data

Get complete family data (nodes + links) for D3 visualization

GET /api/genealogy/people

Get all family members

GET /api/genealogy/:id

Get person by ID with relationships and musical era

GET /api/genealogy/search?q=name

Search people by name

GET /api/genealogy/year/:year/music

Get musical era and family members alive in that year

GET /api/genealogy/musical-timeline

Get complete timeline of family births + musical eras

GET /api/genealogy/stats

Get family tree statistics
