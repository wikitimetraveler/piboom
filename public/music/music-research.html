Music Research Commands
  • "search [artist]" - Search for an artist (e.g., "search Pink Floyd")
  • "tell me about [artist]" - Get detailed artist info (e.g., "tell me about Maynard")
  • "who is [artist]" - Quick artist lookup (e.g., "who is A Perfect Circle")
  • "music research" - Open this research page
AI Chat Commands
  • "ask [question]" - Ask ChatGPT anything (e.g., "ask what makes Pink Floyd unique")
  • "recommend [genre/mood]" - Get music recommendations (e.g., "recommend progressive metal")
  • "explain [topic]" - Get explanations (e.g., "explain the difference between Pink Floyd and Led Zeppelin")
