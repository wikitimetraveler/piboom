How register codes work
  • No. N — a numbered family block in the book (head, wife, children).
  • (N) before a child’s roman numeral — that child heads family No. N later in the register.
  • Pedigree — abbreviated father → grandfather chain printed after the head’s name.
  • Unmatched names stay on a review list; this tool never invents new people.
