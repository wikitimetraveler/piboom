How register codes work
  • No. N — a numbered family block in the book (head, wife, children).
  • (N) before a child’s roman numeral — that child heads family No. N later in the register.
  • Pedigree — abbreviated father → grandfather chain printed after the head’s name.
  • Unmatched OCR crumbs stay on a review list. High-signal register heads/wives/children can be synthesized with book provenance via --enrich.

Deep link examples: ?familyNo=3&section=boston, ?q=Lane&relation=father&bookOnly=1, ?id=10, ?timebuild=1 (auto-start year clock).
