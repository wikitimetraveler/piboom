Quick Tips

  • Tune colors/animations in styles.css.
  • API: /api/audio/files, /api/audio/play/:file, /api/audio/stop, /api/audio/volume/:n, /api/audio/stream/:file.
  • Set MODE=cloud on Render; MODE=pi on the Raspberry Pi.
  • Voice Commands: "search [artist]", "tell me about [artist]", "who is [artist]", "music research", "ask levi [question]"
