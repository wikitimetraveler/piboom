## Typography

- Titles: Georgia, serif — frontier archive
- Kickers: sans, uppercase, letter-spacing 0.22em, sand gold
- Body: system sans

## Mood & motion

- High Desert at dusk — warm radial glows, no cool starfields.
- Ken Burns drift on Mojave Narrows and portrait plate at Lane's Crossing beat.
- Route diagrams on sand-toned plates; crossfades between scenes only.
