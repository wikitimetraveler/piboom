Hadley frontier · Vol. I

Sarah Dickinson
Lane

Wife of Samuel² · Connecticut River household

Lane Genealogies places Samuel Lane in Hadley with Sarah Dickinson and their children. The compilation records her later hardship: widowhood, remarriage to Martin Kellogg, and the 10 February 1704 French and Indian raid — taken captive to Canada with four children. Most returned; one daughter remained. Family narrative from Volume One.
