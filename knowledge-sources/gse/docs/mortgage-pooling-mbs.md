# Mortgage pooling and MBS

## Overview

After a loan closes, lenders often sell it into the secondary market. **Pooling** groups loans with similar features; **securitization** turns that pool into a mortgage-backed security (MBS). Cash flows (principal and interest) pass through to investors.

## Who pools what

- **Fannie Mae / Freddie Mac (GSEs)** — Conventional conforming loans meeting Selling Guide / Seller/Servicer Guide rules. Issue Agency MBS / UMBS. Guaranty is the enterprise’s obligation, not full faith and credit of the U.S. government.
- **Ginnie Mae** — Does not buy loans or underwrite. Guarantees MBS issued by approved issuers that pool government-insured/guaranteed loans (FHA, VA, USDA/RHS). Full faith and credit of the U.S.
- **Private-label** — Non-agency securitizations; outside typical agency TBA liquidity.

## UMBS, TBA, specified pools

- **UMBS** — Uniform MBS under FHFA Single Security; Fannie and Freddie TBA-eligible fixed-rate pools (10/15/20/30-year) are fungible in one TBA market. Re-securitizations of UMBS are called **Supers**.
- **TBA** — Trade agrees on term, coupon, face, price, settlement; pool/CUSIP announced ~48 hours before settle (48-Hour Rule). SIFMA good-delivery rules define interchangeable collateral.
- **Specified pools** — CUSIP known at trade; may pay up vs TBA for desirable prepay traits.

## Fannie Mae pooling parameters (summary)

Confirm current Selling Guide Part C3 before delivery.

- **Single-lender pool** — One lender’s loans. Typical minimum aggregate UPB: $1M fixed-rate; $500K ARMs.
- **Fannie Majors** — Multi-lender; once open, small contributions allowed; often ≤12 months seasoning.
- **Note-rate band (fixed-rate)** — Note rates generally from 25 bp to 112.5 bp above pool pass-through.
- **Term buckets** — Common original-term bands: 30-yr 181–360 mo; 20-yr 181–240; 15-yr 85–180; 10-yr 85–120.
- **Ops** — MBS execution approval, document custodian + Master Custodial Agreement (Form 2017), loan delivery data (often ULDD) with certification before pool close.

## Standard vs same-month pooling (first payment)

- Pool **issue date** is the 1st of the issue month.
- **Standard pooling** — First payment date no later than the 1st of the month immediately after issue (e.g. March 1 issue → first payment on/before April 1).
- **Same-month pooling** — Pool in the same month as origination; first payment may be the 1st of the month two months after issue (e.g. March issue → May 1), with initial interest-only remittance to Fannie because the borrower has not yet paid.
- Separately, first payment must be no later than two months from final disbursement.

## Ginnie Mae pooling (summary)

- **Ginnie Mae I** — Single-issuer pools.
- **Ginnie Mae II** — Custom (single issuer) or multiple-issuer pools from loan packages.
- Pool types include level payment, buydown, ARM, GPM, GEM, serial notes — each with guide chapters and custodian rules.
- Issuers must meet net worth / liquidity tests; electronic pooling required for SF.

## Pass-through economics

Borrower note rate → investor pass-through coupon after servicing fee + guaranty fee (g-fee). Investors receive pro-rata scheduled P&I plus unscheduled principal.

## Verify

Always confirm current Fannie Selling Guide C3, Freddie capital markets / guides, Ginnie Mae MBS Guide, SIFMA TBA guidelines, and investor overlays. Product analyzers do not score pool eligibility or ULDD readiness.
